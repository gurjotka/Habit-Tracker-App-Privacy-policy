Hoppy Habits – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

About this policy

This privacy policy applies to Hoppy Habits, an Android habit-tracking app (package name com.hoppyhabits.app) developed and published on Google Play by Gurjot Kaur ("the developer", "we", "us").

It explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have. You can contact the developer at gjotkofficial17@gmail.com.

In short: Hoppy Habits works entirely on your device. We don't collect, store, share or sell any of your data. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission, so it cannot send your habits anywhere.

Information we collect

None. The developer does not receive or have access to your habits, history, settings or any other information you enter in the app. There are no servers behind the app, and it does not request the internet permission, so it has no way to transmit data off your device.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following in its private storage on your phone. It never leaves your device through the app:

  • The name or nickname you choose to enter (optional)
  • Your habits: names, emojis, colours, schedules, daily targets, units, notes and reminder times
  • Your completion history (including minutes logged with the Focus timer), streaks and statistics
  • Your garden progress and preferences, such as your chosen buddy, outfits, badges, theme and reminder settings

This data is removed when you uninstall the app or choose Reset everything in Settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) – used only to show the habit reminders and the daily check-in you set up yourself. They are scheduled and shown entirely on your device. If you decline this permission the app still works; you just won't get reminders.

The app does not request or use internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or storage, phone, SMS, calendar or advertising ID permissions.

Third parties, ads and analytics

Hoppy Habits contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social-login or other third-party tracking or data-collection services. No data is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party. The app is built only with standard Android and Jetpack libraries that run locally on your device.

Backups and export

  • Android backup: If Android's backup is turned on for your device, Android may back up the app's data (your habits and settings) to your own Google account and restore it when you set up a new phone. This is controlled by you and Google through your device's settings, under Google's privacy policy, and not by the developer. You can turn it off in your device's system settings.
  • Export: The Export feature saves a backup file to a location you choose using Android's system file picker. The file is not encrypted, so anyone who has it can read your habit data. Keep it somewhere safe.
  • Import: The Import feature only reads the single file you select.

Opening the privacy policy

The Privacy Policy link in Settings opens this page in your web browser. Your browser, not the app, makes that connection, and its handling of your visit is governed by your browser's and the hosting provider's own policies.

Your choices and deleting your data

You are in full control of your data. You can archive or delete individual habits inside the app, use Reset everything in Settings to erase all app data, or uninstall the app. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Backup files you exported and any Android backups in your Google account must be deleted by you separately.

Data security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot normally access, and it is never transmitted by the app. Your phone's own screen lock and device encryption are what protect it from other people who may use your phone.

Children's privacy

Hoppy Habits does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes to how the app handles data would also be reflected in the app's Google Play listing.

Contact

Questions or requests about this policy or the Hoppy Habits app (com.hoppyhabits.app)? Contact the developer, Gurjot Kaur, at gjotkofficial17@gmail.com.
