Habit Garden – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

In short: Habit Garden is a habit tracker that works entirely on your device. We don't collect, store, share or sell any of your data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no internet permission, so it cannot send your habits anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. Habit Garden ("the app") does not collect personal information of any kind, and the developer has no access to your habits, history or settings.

What stays on your device

  • The name or nickname you choose to enter (optional)
  • Your habits: names, emojis, colours, schedules, daily targets, notes and reminder times
  • Your completion history, streaks and statistics
  • Your garden progress and preferences: chosen buddy, outfits, badges and settings

All of this lives in the app's private storage on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app or use Reset everything in Settings.

Device permissions

  • Notifications – used only to show the habit reminders you set yourself. Reminders are scheduled and shown on your device, and the app works fine if you decline this permission.

The app does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or storage permissions.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party tracking services, and shares no data with any third party.

Backups and exports

  • If Android's cloud backup is turned on for your device, Android may back up the app's data to your own Google account and restore it on a new phone, according to your device's backup settings. This is controlled by you and Google, not by the app.
  • The Export feature saves a file to a location you choose. Anyone who has that file can read it, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • The Import feature only reads the file you pick.

Deleting your data

Archive or delete individual habits inside the app, use Reset everything in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything from your device. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Exported files and Android backups you created must be deleted by you separately.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with an updated date.
